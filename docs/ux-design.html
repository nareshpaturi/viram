03 / Journeys

Ready first.
Deeper when you want.

A returning practitioner starts with one action. Learning a technique, sharing it, and building routines are optional paths back to the same practice screen.

v1.0

First use

Welcome → Continue → Breathe → Begin

One screen with comfort guidance and the wellness disclaimer. No account, quiz, or permission prompt.

v1.0

Returning

Breathe → Begin → settle → practice → completion

The last practice is ready. The cue chip changes guidance without leaving Breathe.

v1.0

Learn a technique

Practices → guide → Begin → introduction → practice

Take care is visible before Begin. Adjust rhythm and Share are secondary.

v1.0

Share with students

Guide or My rhythms → Share → link → preview → Save or Begin

Without the app, the link opens a small web page with store links. A link never starts practice by itself.

v1.0

Eyes closed, phone locked

Begin → lock the phone → voice guides → a call pauses → Resume

Voice and Tones keep going on the audio clock. Calls, other apps' audio, and headphone disconnects pause with a reason.

v1.0

Quick start

Long-press icon → shortcut → settle → practice

Cancel during settling leaves no record. First use still comes first.

v1.1

Program

Programs → overview → plan → Breathe shows the next session → session dots

Sessions, not days. A missed day never resets progress; returning offers repeat or continue.

v1.1

Routine

Routine → practice → transition → next practice → summary

One pause, end, and interruption model covers the whole routine.

Duration contract: round seconds = the sum of a practice's steps (a hold set to Off counts as 0). Planned rounds = round up (target seconds ÷ round seconds). Planned time = rounds × round seconds. Box 4 · 4 · 4 · 4 at 5 min = 19 rounds / 5:04; Nadi Shodhana in 4 · out 6 each side = 15 rounds / 5:00; 4-7-8 at its 4-round default = 1:16. Introductions, settling, transitions, and paused time never count as practice time. Guided pace is inhale steps per minute, always labeled “guided.”

05 / Guidance & accessibility

The rhythm stays predictable.

The guide supports each step; the timer stays authoritative. Techniques add side, hum, and half-second cues without adding colors.

Inhale

Sky. Expand from the small resting size to the full guide across the step.

Hold after inhale

Saffron. Full and still. Skipped entirely when set to Off.

Exhale · Hum

Coral. Contract to the resting size. Hum is an exhale with its own label and glyph.

Rest

Mist. Small and still: the pause after exhaling. Skipped when Off.

LeftRight

Side cues

Filled means open; a crossed outline means closed. The step label and voice always name the side. Sides are shown from the practitioner's point of view.

Hum

Bhramari's exhale is labeled “Hum” with a soft wave under the guide. The caption explains the sound once; voice says “Hum.”

Half-second rhythms

Coherent breathing shows the guide and a progress ring instead of a numeral. The caption states the rhythm in seconds.

Voice cues

One calm AI voice, generated once and bundled. Cue words are at most 0.8 s and start on the boundary. Introductions play before settling, with captions.

Motion follows the count.

A gentle continuous curve, no overshoot or decorative pulsing. Pause freezes guidance. Reduced motion keeps a fixed-size guide with labels, counts, voice, tones, and haptics.

Every cue has a fallback.

Screen readers hear the step and seconds once per boundary (“Inhale left, 4 seconds”), not every second. Practice works visual-only, voice-only, tone-only, or haptic-only.

Locked screen: Voice and Tones keep guiding on the audio clock; the visual guide re-syncs on return. A session keeps going with the screen locked when a non-visual cue can still play: Voice or Tones on both platforms, or haptics on Android. Otherwise (Silent without haptics, or Silent with haptics on iOS, where background haptics are not allowed) it pauses on lock. Lock-screen controls never show more than practice name, round, time left, and Pause/Resume/End.
Accessibility handoff: 48 × 48 mock targets; 52 px primary controls; visible focus; scalable text; scrollable content; safe-area insets. Never truncate a step label, safety message, or action. Drag-to-reorder in routines needs Move up / Move down actions for assistive technology. Verify VoiceOver/TalkBack, reduced motion, and 100/150/200% text on devices during implementation.
