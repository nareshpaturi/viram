01

Executive summary

What ships, who it serves, and how to tell if it works.

→
Product promise

Authentic pranayama, guided at your pace. Free, offline, no account. A practitioner can choose a named technique, learn how it is traditionally done, adjust its rhythm, and follow it with eyes closed. A beginner never has to build anything: choose a technique, or press Begin, and follow.

1.0
Release rule

v1.0 is blocked only by the trustworthy practice loop, the eight sourced techniques, bundled voice cues, guidance that continues with the screen locked, sound controls, a minutes or rounds target, My rhythms, safe share links, quick start, one-screen first use, data export and backup, the free-core promise, accessibility, and privacy. Curated programs, night practice, gradual slowing, routines, gentle progression, the practice calendar, the daily reminder, Health writing, and fuller voice are v1.1. Revenue is intentionally out of scope for now.

MVP at a glance · v1.0

From the user needs research (about 10,900 App Store and Google Play reviews): people punish paywalls and broken trust, praise simplicity, and ask most for sound control, custom patterns, eyes-closed and locked-screen guidance, and rounds.

Jobs to be done

  • When I learn a technique, show me how to do it properly—its name, pronunciation, steps, context, and care notes—then let me follow it.
  • When I practice with my eyes closed, guide every step with voice, or with tones and haptics.
  • When I have a minute, let me start instantly from the home screen.
  • When I teach, let me share a practice with my students so they follow the rhythm I set.
  • When I finish, keep a private record of my practice on this device.

Practice engine · step sequence

A practice is an ordered sequence of steps. Each step has a kind, a length in seconds, and an optional side. The v2 fixed four-phase loop becomes the custom builder’s four rows.

Inhale1–20 s
Hold0–20 s · 0 = Off
Exhale1–20 s
Rest0–20 s · 0 = Off
step = { kind: inhale | hold | exhale | rest, seconds, side?: left | right } bounds: inhale, exhale 1–20 s · hold, rest 0–20 s (0 = skipped, shown “Off”) custom builder: exactly four steps, whole seconds library: half seconds allowed; more than four steps; side labels; content may name a cue variant or route for a step (Hum on the Bhramari exhale; mouth on the Sheetali inhale and the 4-7-8 exhale) Sama Vritti [inhale 4] [hold 4] [exhale 4] [rest 4] Visama Vritti [inhale 4] [hold Off] [exhale 6] [rest Off] Nadi Shodhana [inhale 4 left] [exhale 6 right] [inhale 4 right] [exhale 6 left] Coherent [inhale 5.5] [exhale 5.5]

Duration contract

Round
One pass through a practice’s steps; a Nadi Shodhana round covers both sides. “Round” replaces the v2 term “cycle.” Round length = sum of step seconds; 0-second steps add nothing and are skipped.
Planned rounds
Planned rounds = round up (target seconds ÷ round seconds). Show planned rounds and planned duration before Begin. Box 4 · 4 · 4 · 4 at 5 minutes is 19 rounds / 5:04; 4 · 4 · 6 · 4 is 17 rounds / 5:06; 4-7-8 at its default of 4 rounds is 1:16.
Guided pace
Shown as guided breaths per minute: inhale steps per minute, calculated from the rhythm. Box 4 · 4 · 4 · 4 is 3.8; Nadi Shodhana in 4 · out 6 each side is 6. Never presented as a measured rate.
Targets
1, 3, 5, or 10 minutes, or 1–108 rounds (FR-01). Each library technique sets its default target (table below); 4-7-8 defaults to 4 rounds, the others to minutes. Targets are not exact cutoffs; the round that reaches the target finishes.
Routines · v1.1
Planned duration = sum of segments; each segment rounds up to whole rounds of its own practice. Example: box 3 min (12 rounds / 3:12) then coherent 5 min (28 rounds / 5:08) = 8:20. Transition screens are not practice time.
Start
Optional voice introduction (skippable), then a three-second neutral settle countdown. Cancel returns without a session record. Introductions and countdowns are not practice time.
Pause
Pause immediately; resume restarts the interrupted step after a 3-second countdown, preserving completed rounds and prior active time.
End early
End pauses guidance and asks “End this practice?” Keep breathing uses the resume countdown; dismissal leaves the practice paused. End session stores actual active time and complete rounds locally as “Ended early,” including zero rounds.
Half-second steps
Show the guide and a progress ring instead of a countdown numeral; coherent breathing is followed, not counted.
Completion
Soft completion cue, practice name, rhythm, actual active time, completed rounds, guided breaths per minute, and local-save result. Primary “Done,” secondary “Breathe again.” v1.1 adds the progression offer, “Make it easier next time,” and a separate Health status.

04

Functional requirements

Testable behavior for the product and engineering team. FR-01 to FR-13, FR-21, and FR-22 are v1.0; FR-14 to FR-20 and FR-23 to FR-25 are v1.1.

FR-01 · Rhythm configuration
  • Breathe shows the last or default practice and the primary Begin action first. Adjust rhythm opens a dedicated surface; Use this rhythm returns to Breathe. Adjusting is never a required step.
  • A rhythm is an ordered list of steps. Kinds: inhale, hold, exhale, rest; optional side: left or right.
  • Inhale and exhale are 1–20 s. Hold and rest are 0–20 s; 0 skips the step and displays “Off.” 4 · 4 · 6 · 4 and 4 · 0 · 6 · 0 are valid.
  • The custom builder has four rows (Inhale, Hold, Exhale, Rest) in whole seconds using accessible steppers.
  • Library techniques may define half-second steps and more than four steps with side labels. Adjusting a library technique keeps its step structure and sides; values move in whole seconds, or half seconds where the technique defines them, within the same bounds.
  • Changing any value immediately updates round length, planned rounds, planned duration, and guided breaths per minute.
  • The target is either minutes (1, 3, 5, or 10) or rounds (1–108, with 11, 21, and 27 as shortcuts). Planned rounds and planned duration are shown for both before Begin. v1.1: an option for half-second steps in the custom builder.
  • The last practice and its settings persist locally; invalid or free-text values cannot be entered.
FR-02 · Timer accuracy
  • Use a monotonic clock and calculate the current step from elapsed time; never rely on chained one-second intervals.
  • The visual guide updates smoothly while enabled voice, tone, or haptic cues fire once per non-zero step boundary. 0-second steps are skipped without a cue.
  • With the screen locked in Voice or Tones mode, cues stay within ±250 ms; a 5-minute 4 · 4 · 4 · 4 practice run entirely with the screen locked finishes within ±250 ms of 5:04.
  • After an automatic pause (FR-04) or a user pause, remain visibly paused. Resume restarts the interrupted step after three neutral seconds, preserving completed rounds and prior active time. Pauses, settle countdowns, introductions, and routine transitions do not count toward practice time.
  • A 5-minute target at 4 · 4 · 4 · 4 finishes within ±250 ms of the disclosed 5:04 in uninterrupted device tests; coherent breathing at 5 minutes finishes within ±250 ms of 5:08.
FR-03 · Guidance cues
  • Cue mode is Voice, Tones, or Silent, changed from the cue chip on Breathe or in Settings. Haptics toggle independently. Defaults: Voice on, haptics on, motion follows the system.
  • Voice uses AI-generated clips bundled in the app: cue words Inhale, Hold, Exhale, Rest, and Hum; side phrases Inhale left, Inhale right, Exhale left, and Exhale right; and route phrases In through the mouth and Out through the mouth. No runtime speech generation, API, key, or network dependency.
  • Cue words are at most 0.8 s, side phrases 1.2 s, and route phrases 1.6 s. A step with a side speaks its side phrase. A mouth step speaks its route phrase on the first round and its cue word after that; the screen shows the route every round. When a step is shorter than its cue plus 0.2 s, that step plays its tone instead.
  • When Voice is on, voice replaces tones at step boundaries; haptics stay independent. Silent plays no audio. The completion cue is a soft tone in Voice and Tones modes.
  • Optional technique introductions play before the settle countdown, with captions, and can be skipped. Settings offers First time (default), Always, or Never.
  • In Tones and Silent modes, side labels stay visible and are announced to screen readers.
  • Haptic patterns are recognizable without being startling; unsupported devices degrade silently. A missing or unplayable clip falls back to that step’s tone.
  • Sound controls: a cue volume separate from media volume; “Play alongside other audio” (Viram mixes and, on Android, lowers other audio briefly during cues) or “Pause other audio”, the default on iOS so the lock-screen controls appear, with “Play alongside” the default on Android (decided September 27, 2026); tone sets Soft bells (default), Wood, and Chimes, each with distinct inhale, hold, exhale, and rest sounds; haptic strength Light, Medium (default), or Strong. All persist.
  • Silent-switch behavior follows the CP1 locked decision. Viram never stops other audio unless “Pause other audio” is chosen or is the iOS default.
FR-04 · Locked screen and interruptions
  • In Voice or Tones mode, guidance continues when the screen locks or the app moves to the background. Practice time counts. On return, the visual guide re-syncs from the same clock.
  • Automatic pauses: phone or VoIP calls, Siri, alarms, another app taking audio focus without mixing, headphones or Bluetooth audio disconnecting, and Pause from the lock-screen controls. On return, the paused state names the reason (e.g. “Paused for a call”) and never shows a stale running timer.
  • Silent mode: A session keeps going with the screen locked when a non-visual cue can still play: Voice or Tones on both platforms, or haptics on Android. Otherwise (Silent without haptics, or Silent with haptics on iOS, where background haptics are not allowed) it pauses on lock.
  • Lock-screen controls: iOS Now Playing shows the practice name, round, and time left, with Pause and Resume. Android shows a media-style notification from a mediaPlayback foreground service with Pause, Resume, and End. No Live Activity in v1.0.
  • Screen: with Voice or Tones, the screen follows the system auto-lock. “Keep screen on during practice” is off by default; in Silent mode the screen always stays on during practice.
  • The settle screen tells people once that they can lock the phone and the voice will keep guiding.
  • A voice clip interrupted mid-play does not resume on its own. Force-quit during a session does not create a completed record. The audio session is released when practice ends.
FR-05 · Session history
  • Only completed or intentionally ended sessions are stored.
  • Each record includes start time, actual active duration, practice ID (library technique ID, saved rhythm ID, or custom), practice-name snapshot, step snapshot (kind, seconds, and side for every step), target type and value (minutes or rounds), completed rounds, guided breaths per minute, and completion state.
  • Records keep their snapshots when a saved rhythm is renamed or deleted or when library content is updated.
  • User can delete all local history from Settings after an in-page confirmation step. Preferences and My rhythms are not deleted.
  • From v1.1, records also carry Health-write status, and deletion explains that records already written to Apple Health or Health Connect are not deleted.
FR-06 · Offline behavior
  • Every practice feature works in airplane mode after install, including library content, voice clips, pronunciation clips, and decoding a share link.
  • No account, network request, remote content, or server is required to begin or complete a practice. Only the web fallback page and store links need a network.
  • From v1.1, Health writes queue locally if the platform API is temporarily unavailable and retry on the next app open.
FR-07 · Safety language
  • First use says to stop and return to normal breathing if lightheaded or uncomfortable, and shows the wellness disclaimer.
  • Settings includes a durable wellness—not medical advice—statement.
  • No copy promises to treat anxiety, disease, insomnia, or any medical condition. No treatment, diagnosis, or outcome claims.
  • No hold longer than the selected count; holds never exceed 20 s in v1.x; no competitive scoring.
  • Guided breaths per minute are always labelled as guided, never as a measured rate.
FR-08 · Practice library
  • Practices lists exactly eight gentle techniques in v1.0: Sama Vritti (box), Visama Vritti (extended exhale), Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril, no retention), Bhramari (humming bee), Ujjayi (ocean breath), Sheetali (cooling; Sheetkari alternative), coherent breathing (5.5 · 5.5), and 4-7-8.
  • Each technique ships with the default rhythm and target in the library defaults table.
  • Rows show the romanized and English name; My rhythms follow the library.
  • Nadi Shodhana steps carry left/right side labels; the Bhramari exhale is cued as Hum; Sheetali offers Sheetkari for people who cannot roll the tongue.
  • Technique content is bundled, versioned data. No technique is downloaded, locked, or paid.
FR-09 · Technique content and safety
  • Each technique has a romanized name (common romanization, no diacritics), English name, pronunciation respelling and pronunciation clip, how-to steps, traditional context, “Take care” guidance, “What research says,” and “Based on” sources.
  • “Take care” is visible on technique detail before Begin and is never hidden behind a collapsed control.
  • Tradition is framed as tradition (“Traditionally practiced to…”). Evidence is stated plainly, including null results.
  • “Reviewed by [name, credential]” appears only when a real review is recorded in the content data. No other copy implies review.
  • Only gentle-tier techniques ship in v1.x. Defaults sit below classical ratios, and holds never exceed 20 s.
FR-10 · My rhythms
  • Save up to 20 named rhythms from the custom builder, an adjusted library technique, or a received link.
  • Names are plain text, 1–40 characters.
  • Rename and delete are local; delete asks for in-page confirmation. Deleting a rhythm leaves history records unchanged.
  • At 20 saved rhythms, Save explains the limit and routes to My rhythms; nothing is overwritten silently.
  • A rhythm based on a library technique keeps its technique ID, so its name variants, cues, and “Take care” come from the installed library.
FR-11 · Share links
  • Share a library technique’s current settings or a saved rhythm as https://viram.app/r/… through the system share sheet, after a share preview that shows exactly what the link contains.
  • A link carries only: a display name (plain text, at most 40 characters), a step list (kind, seconds within bounds, optional side), a target (1, 3, 5, or 10 minutes, or 1–108 rounds), and an optional library technique ID. No history, personal data, or device identifier.
  • Validate every field and reject the whole link if anything is missing, malformed, unknown, or out of bounds. Without a technique ID, the step list must fit the custom builder (four steps, whole seconds). With one, it must match that installed technique’s step structure.
  • Links cannot unlock techniques that are not in the installed library; an unknown technique ID is invalid. Vigorous techniques are not shareable in v1.x.
  • Never render link content as HTML, in the app or on the web page. Safety and instruction text always comes from the app’s own content, never from a link.
  • A valid link opens an in-app preview with Save and Begin. Begin goes through the settle countdown with Cancel. Nothing is saved automatically. If first use is incomplete, first use comes first, then the preview.
  • An invalid link shows “This link can’t be opened” with a route to Practices. Nothing is partially imported and the app never crashes.
  • Without the app, the same URL serves a static web page that shows the rhythm as plain text with App Store and Google Play links. The page has no trackers; the teacher-loop signal comes from aggregate host logs and store campaign attribution.
FR-12 · Quick start
  • App-icon quick actions on iOS and Android: “Begin last practice” (its subtitle names the most recent practice), “1-minute box breathing,” and the most recent other practice (for example, “Bhramari · 5 min”). With fewer than two distinct practices in history, the third action is omitted.
  • Each action opens the settle countdown with Cancel; Cancel returns to Breathe without a record. Introductions are skipped.
  • Before first use is complete, every quick action opens first use.
  • If the most recent practice no longer exists (for example, a deleted saved rhythm), “Begin last practice” opens Breathe with the default practice.
  • Actions work from a cold start and while the app is in the background.
FR-13 · First use
  • First use is one screen: welcome, comfort guidance, wellness disclaimer, and Continue.
  • No permission prompt, cue setup, account, or Health invitation appears in first use.
  • “Continue” records first use as complete and opens Breathe with Sama Vritti at 5 minutes ready. “Begin” is reserved for starting a practice. When a quick action or link opened first use, Continue proceeds to that settle countdown or link preview instead.
  • Cue choices live in the cue chip on Breathe and in Settings. Settings repeats the comfort guidance.
FR-14 · Routines · v1.1

  • Pause, end, and interruption rules apply to the whole routine. Ending during a segment records the completed segments and the partial one as ended early.
  • The summary lists each practice completed.
FR-15 · Gentle progression · v1.1

  • Actions: Try it / Not now / Stop suggesting. Progression is never automatic.
  • “Make it easier next time” is always available after a practice.

FR-16 · Practice calendar · v1.1

FR-17 · Daily reminder · v1.1

  • If permission is denied, the setting says so and stays off; no repeated prompting.
FR-18 · Health session writing · v1.1

Local practice is complete on its own. iOS writes the completed mindful session through HealthKit, named “Apple Health” in copy; Android writes the supported session record through Health Connect after detecting availability. No biometric reads. The v2 states below are unchanged.

Eligibility and consent

  1. Save a qualifying completed practice locally first: at least 60 seconds of active practice, never intentionally ended early.
  2. Keep Done and Breathe again primary in the summary. Offer “Add sessions to [platform]” as a separate optional action; Settings provides a later entry point.
  3. Explain session writing only, then open the real system permission UI. No prompt on first launch or during first use.
  4. Write only after platform authorization permits it. A completed permission request is not a confirmed write.
  5. After skipping, declining, failure, or unavailability, retain all core breathing and local-history behavior.

Result states

Not connected / skipped
“Saved here, as always.” Offer a later connection in Settings without repeated prompting.
Pending
“Waiting to add your session.” Local history remains available; retry transient failures safely.
Written
“Added to [platform].” Show only after a confirmed write.
Unavailable
“Health is unavailable.” No claim about wearable ownership or read access.
Failed
“Couldn’t add this session.” Retain the local record and provide a retry route in Settings.

Acceptance criteria

  • One local practice creates at most one platform record; retries are idempotent.
  • Sub-60-second and intentionally ended sessions never export.
  • No read permissions, biometric placeholders, or insight screens appear.
  • Deleting local history leaves preferences and existing platform records untouched; explain this before confirmation.
  • Validate availability, restrictions, revocation, pending/failure handling, and actual write results on both physical platforms.

Content & safety governance

Technique content is product surface with safety consequences. These rules decide what ships.

Risk tier · gentle

May ship with sourced content

The v1.0 eight. Ship with sourced content and gentle defaults. Shareable.

Risk tier · vigorous or retention-led

Needs a named instructor review

Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, long kumbhaka, bandhas. A named human instructor review is required before they ship; rapid-rhythm mode also needs a pre-practice safety check. Not shareable in v1.x.

Sources and review

  1. Claude drafts technique content from classical and modern sources. Every technique lists its sources under “Based on.”
  2. Each draft is checked against its listed sources before release; the check is recorded with the content version.
  3. “Reviewed by [name, credential]” appears only after a real review is recorded (reviewer, credential, date, content version). Never imply review.

Framing rules

  • Frame tradition as tradition: “Traditionally practiced to…”.
  • State evidence plainly, including null results such as the Sussex coherent-breathing trial.
  • No treatment, diagnosis, or outcome claims, in the app, on the web page, or in store copy.

Gentle defaults

  • Library defaults sit below classical ratios.
  • Holds never exceed 20 s in v1.x. Nadi Shodhana ships without retention.
  • Progression paths (v1.1) stay within the same bounds and are never automatic.

Content data contract

Identity
id, contentVersion, riskTier, shareable, family (classical or modern).
Names
Name (common romanization, no diacritics) and subtitle (English name). For Sanskrit names: Devanagari, pronunciation respelling, and pronunciation clip reference.
Practice
Default steps (kind, seconds, side, optional Hum cue or mouth route, and a one-line caption), adjustment increment (whole or half seconds), default target (minutes or rounds), posture, optional progression path (v1.1).

06

AI voice & names

One calm voice, generated once, bundled, and checked by ear.

AI voice requirements

  1. Generate once (ElevenLabs or equivalent) under a commercial licence, then bundle the clips. No runtime API, key, or network dependency.
  2. One calm, consistent voice across cue words, introductions, and pronunciation clips.
  3. Cue words are at most 0.8 s, side phrases 1.2 s, and route phrases 1.6 s. When a step is shorter than its cue plus 0.2 s, play that step’s tone instead.
  4. Voice replaces tones when on; haptics stay independent. Platform audio conventions follow the CP1 locked decision.
  5. Sanskrit names use a pronunciation dictionary. A listener who knows Sanskrit or Hindi approves every clip before release; text review alone cannot validate pronunciation.
  6. Settings → About discloses that voice guidance is AI-generated.
  7. Keep the licence, generation settings, pronunciation dictionary, and clip manifest with the release evidence.

Clip inventory

Cues · v1.0
Words: Inhale, Hold, Exhale, Rest, Hum. Side phrases: Inhale left, Inhale right, Exhale left, Exhale right. Route phrases: In through the mouth, Out through the mouth. Scripts in src/content/voice.ts.
Introductions · v1.0
One optional short introduction per technique, played before practice.
Pronunciation · v1.0
One clip per technique name, played from technique detail.
Fuller voice · v1.1
Optional counting and longer introductions.

Names and typography

The bundled Newsreader and DM Sans files contain macrons and ś but lack the IAST underdot and overdot letters (ḍ ṣ ṭ ṇ ṃ ḥ ṛ), verified from each font’s character map. The UI therefore uses common romanization without diacritics (Nadi Shodhana, Bhramari), a pronunciation respelling (“NAH-dee SHOH-duh-nuh”), and a pronunciation clip. Full IAST requires adding a covering font such as Noto Serif, which is a future brand decision.

Brand name: Viram (विराम, “a pause”), said “vee-RAHM.” Renamed from Sama on September 23, 2026 because “Sama Breath” was already on the App Store; see the naming exploration. Trademark clearance is a prelaunch item (12).

Screen readers receive the respelling as the accessible hint for each technique name.

Technical architecture

One cross-platform codebase, bundled content, and no backend beyond a static site.

Framework

React Native with Expo development builds (Expo SDK 57), expo-router, and TypeScript. Expo Go alone is insufficient for the native modules.

Recommended
Visual system

The branding board owns palette, Newsreader/DM Sans typography, logo, and launch identity; the UX mocks own screen hierarchy and state behavior. Runtime alignment to the v3 docs is future work; this revision is not implementation evidence.

Locked
Navigation

Four expo-router tabs: Breathe · Practices · History · Settings. Technique detail, Adjust rhythm, practice, completion, and link preview are stack routes.

Four tabs
Practice engine

A pure step-sequence engine: ordered steps (kind, seconds, optional side), 0-second steps skipped, half seconds allowed, time from a monotonic clock. Animation, voice, tones, and haptics read engine state and never own time.

Pure engine
Audio engine

Cues are scheduled on a native audio timeline driven by the audio clock, not JavaScript timers, so guidance stays accurate with the screen locked. iOS: background audio mode (justified by audible guidance), interruption and route-change handling, Now Playing controls; the microphone stays disabled. Android: a mediaPlayback foreground service with a media session, audio focus, and a media-style notification. The session is released after practice.

Native timeline
Backups and export

The SQLite file lives where iOS backs it up and Android Auto Backup includes it. Export and import use a versioned JSON schema validated in CI. No server.

Device-owned
Technique content

Bundled, versioned structured data validated by a schema test in CI. No remote content. Sessions snapshot their steps, so content updates never rewrite history.

Bundled data
Audio

Bundled voice clips and tones through expo-audio. A build-time clip manifest records each clip’s measured length for the cue-plus-0.2 s fallback rule.

Bundled
Deep links

expo-router handles https://viram.app/r/… as a route. iOS Universal Links (Associated Domains) and Android App Links (verified intent filter), with apple-app-site-association and assetlinks.json hosted on viram.app. The payload is decoded and validated on device.

Verified links
Web fallback

A static page at viram.app/r/* decodes the link in the browser and renders plain text only, with store links. No cookies, trackers, or analytics scripts; aggregate host logs only.

Static site
Quick actions

iOS Home Screen quick actions and Android app shortcuts. Candidate library: expo-quick-actions. This is an assumption to verify against the installed Expo SDK 57 (config plugin, cold and warm launch routing) before CP2b starts.

Verify
State and persistence

Pure engine plus a small settings store. expo-sqlite for sessions, My rhythms, and routines (v1.1), with schema-versioned migrations from day one.

Device only
Backend

None. The only hosted surface is the static viram.app site (link files, web fallback, privacy policy). Optional privacy-preserving analytics only with explicit policy disclosure.

No server
Native boundaries · v1.1

HealthKit and Health Connect sit behind one platform-neutral HealthRepository for session writing and deduplication; no reads, no vendor wearable SDKs. The daily reminder schedules one local notification.

Writes only

Suggested module structure

Engine model

stepAt(elapsedMs, steps): active = steps where seconds > 0 // 0-s holds and rests are skipped roundMs = sum(active.seconds × 1000) // half seconds allowed rounds = floor(elapsedMs ÷ roundMs) position = elapsedMs mod roundMs index = first cumulative boundary greater than position remaining = that boundary − position plannedRounds = ceil(targetSeconds ÷ roundSeconds) guidedBreathsPerMinute = 60 × inhaleStepsPerRound ÷ roundSeconds cueFor(step, mode): Silent → none Voice and step ≥ cue clip length + 0.2 s → voice clip otherwise → the step’s tone Box 4·4·4·4, 5 min → 16 s rounds, 19 rounds / 5:04, 3.8 breaths/min Nadi Shodhana 4·6 ×2 → 20 s rounds, 15 rounds / 5:00, 6 breaths/min Coherent 5.5·5.5, 5 min → 11 s rounds, 28 rounds / 5:08 Pause stores step index, completed rounds, and active duration. Resume restarts that step after a neutral countdown; wall time is not practice time.

09

Quality bar

A practice app fails when timing, cues, content, or links are untrustworthy.

Performance
  • Cold launch to usable Breathe under 2 seconds on target devices.
  • 60 fps guide animation on mid-range devices.
  • Voice and tone cues start on the step boundary.
  • No network dependency for any practice flow.
Accessibility
  • WCAG AA contrast.
  • Dynamic text without clipping, including technique content.
  • VoiceOver/TalkBack step and side announcements.
  • Reduced-motion alternative, including the progress ring.
Reliability
  • Crash-free session target ≥99.5%.
  • Deterministic engine, record, and link tests.
  • Full practice flow with no permissions or network.
  • No duplicate Health records (v1.1).

Test suites

Engine
0-second holds skipped without cues, side labels in order, half-second steps, round-up duration math for every library default, pause/resume, and (v1.1) per-segment routine rounding.
Voice
Every cue clip measured within its limit (words 0.8 s, side phrases 1.2 s, route phrases 1.6 s); tone fallback when a step is shorter than its cue plus 0.2 s; voice, tone, haptic, and visual stay in sync; clip manifest matches content; every clip approved for pronunciation by a Sanskrit or Hindi listener.
Links
Encode/decode round trip; validation of every field; fuzz tests with malformed, truncated, oversized, out-of-bounds, unknown-ID, and HTML- or script-bearing input; no HTML rendering in the app or on the web page.
Content
Schema validation; every technique has sources, “Take care,” and “What research says”; holds ≤ 20 s; no “Reviewed by” without a review record; only gentle-tier techniques present.
Device checks
Universal Links and App Links on physical devices (installed and not installed, cold and warm start); quick actions on both platforms (cold and warm, before first use, after the referenced rhythm is deleted).

Device test matrix

iOS
Oldest supported iOS version on a smaller iPhone; current iOS on standard and large-screen iPhones; VoiceOver; silent mode; reduced motion; Universal Links; Home Screen quick actions.
Android
Oldest supported Android version, one mid-range physical device, current Pixel, one Samsung device; TalkBack; battery saver; App Links verification; app shortcuts.
Links
Opened from common messaging and browser apps with and without the app installed; first use incomplete; invalid link.
Interruptions
Call, VoIP call, Siri, alarm, another app starting audio (mixable and non-mixable), headphones and Bluetooth disconnect, lock-screen Pause and Resume, voice clip mid-play, low battery, timezone change.
Locked screen
Voice and Tones sessions of 5 and 20 minutes with the screen locked from the first step: cue drift within ±250 ms, correct practice time, correct rounds, and lock-screen controls. Includes Android battery saver and Doze, and iOS Low Power Mode. Silent mode pauses on lock.
Data safety
Upgrade from every released schema; kill the app during a write; restore from an iOS backup and Android Auto Backup; export, then import into a clean install and into a populated one with no duplicates.
Voice gate
At least 8 listeners, including pranayama practitioners and a Sanskrit or Hindi speaker, rate calm, pace, and naturalness; the median must be at least 4/5.
Health write · v1.1
No connection, unavailable platform, declined/restricted write, pending, failed, revoked, and confirmed write. Local practice works throughout.

Release blockers

  • Timer or step cue drifts by more than one second.
  • A 0-second step plays a cue, or a side label is wrong.
  • Link content is rendered unsafely (as HTML or markup), or an out-of-bounds rhythm is accepted from a link.
  • A vigorous or retention-led technique ships without a recorded named instructor review.
  • “Reviewed by” is shown without a recorded review.
  • A voice clip ships without pronunciation approval, or About does not disclose AI-generated voice.
  • Copy makes a treatment, diagnosis, or outcome claim, or labels guided pace as measured.
  • Session cannot be paused or safely ended.
  • In Voice or Tones mode, guidance stops or drifts beyond ±250 ms with the screen locked, or continues through a call.
  • An update, migration, or import loses or duplicates history or My rhythms.
  • Any ad, sign-in, paywall on core practice, or rating prompt during or right after practice.
  • Core guidance is unusable with screen reader or reduced motion.
  • Any permission is requested before context is explained.
  • Privacy policy and store disclosures disagree with actual SDK behavior.
  • v1.1: app records duplicate, zero-length, or incorrect-time Health sessions.
