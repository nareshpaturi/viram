Why Viram

Guidance, even locked

A calm voice or soft tones mark every step and keep going with the screen off. In Silent mode, haptics guide on Android; on iPhone you choose soft tones or a pause.

At your pace

Any length from 1 to 60 minutes, or a set number of rounds. Adjust a rhythm, save your own, or follow a program.

Private and offline

No account and no ads. Viram doesn’t collect your practice history: it stays on your phone, and every practice works without a connection.

Also in the app: routines, a beginner program, night practice, haptics that differ by phase, share links, and Apple Health or Health Connect if you choose.

How it works

Choose, settle, breathe.

  1. Choose a practice

    Begin with the practice ready on Breathe, or pick one of twelve from the library. Each has its own guide.

  2. Settle in

    A short spoken introduction the first time, then three quiet seconds, with the technique’s care note before you start.

  3. Follow the guide

    The voice, tones, or haptics mark every step. Pause anytime, and your practice is saved to History when you finish.

Privacy

Your practice stays yours.

Practising safely

  • Breathe gently. If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, stop and return to your normal breath.
  • Chest pain, fainting, or a pounding heart with dizziness: stop and get medical help.
  • Don’t practise while driving or operating machinery.
  • Never practise breath holds in or near water, including the bath.
  • Living with a heart or lung condition, or pregnant? Check with your clinician first, especially before holds.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Viram free?

Yes. Viram is free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and no account.

Does it keep guiding with my phone locked?

With voice or tones, yes: Viram keeps guiding with the screen off, so you can put your phone down and close your eyes. In Silent mode, Android keeps guiding with haptics; an iPhone can’t play haptics while locked, so you choose whether locking pauses or continues with soft tones.

What does Viram know about me?

Viram doesn’t collect your practice history, and there’s no account or server. Your history stays on your phone, in your phone’s own backups, and in any file you export. If you turn them on, Apple Health or Health Connect receive each finished practice’s start and end time, and a paired watch gets your practices. Privacy policy.

Is breathing practice safe?

Viram teaches gentle practices, without forceful holds, and every technique has its own care notes. Breathe gently and stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable. If you live with a heart or lung condition or are pregnant, check with your clinician first. Viram is a wellness practice, not medical advice.
