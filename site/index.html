How a practice works

Three steps, then just breathing.

  1. Choose a practice

    Start with Breathe’s ready practice, pick from the library, or open one of your own rhythms.

  2. Settle in

    A short spoken introduction the first time, then three quiet seconds to settle before the first breath.

  3. Breathe with the guide

    The voice, tones, or haptics mark every step. Pause anytime; your time is saved to History when you finish.

Practising safely

Gentle by design.

Viram teaches gentle forms of pranayama, without forceful breath holds, and every technique carries its own care notes. These apply to all of them:

  • Stop and breathe normally if you feel dizzy, tingly, short of breath, or anxious.
  • Chest pain, fainting, or a pounding heart with dizziness: stop and get medical help.
  • Don’t practise while driving or operating machinery.
  • Never practise breath holds in or near water, including the bath.
  • Living with a heart or lung condition, or pregnant? Check with your clinician first, especially before holds.
  • If focusing on your breath makes you more anxious, stop. That happens to some people and is normal.
  • Breathing practice is not a treatment and doesn’t replace medical care.

Questions

Good to know.

What is pranayama?

Pranayama is the practice of shaping the breath, from the yoga tradition. Its techniques set the length of each inhale, exhale, and pause, sometimes with humming, alternate nostrils, or a soft sound in the throat. Viram teaches gentle forms, without forceful holds.

Is Viram free?

Yes. Viram is free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and no account.

Does it keep guiding with my phone locked?

Yes. With voice or tones, Viram keeps guiding with the screen off, so you can put your phone down and close your eyes. In Silent mode, Android keeps guiding with haptics; an iPhone can’t play haptics while locked, so you choose whether locking pauses or continues with soft tones.

Does Viram work offline?

Yes. Everything, including the voice, is on your phone. Viram never needs a connection to guide a practice.

Which breathing techniques are included?

Sama Vritti (box breathing), Visama Vritti (extended exhale), Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing, also called Anulom Vilom), Bhramari (humming bee breath), Ujjayi (ocean breath), Sheetali (cooling breath), coherent breathing, 4-7-8 breathing, Dirgha (three-part breath), Udgeeth (Om chanting), Chandra Bhedana (left nostril breathing), and cyclic sighing. Read the guides.

What does Viram know about me?

Nothing leaves your phone. There is no account and no server. Your practice history stays on your device, and you can export or delete it anytime. Privacy policy.

Can I share a practice with my students?

Yes. Any practice, including a rhythm you adjusted, can be sent as a link. It opens in Viram ready to begin, and the link carries only the practice, never anything about you.

Is breathing practice safe?

Viram teaches gentle practices, and every technique has its own care notes. Breathe gently and stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable. If you live with a heart or lung condition or are pregnant, check with your clinician first. Viram is a wellness practice, not medical advice.
